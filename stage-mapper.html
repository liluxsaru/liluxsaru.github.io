Platforms & export
Pinch with two fingers to zoom in or out. Drag a platform body to move it, its end-dots to resize. The background art stays put once loaded - drag the cyan ground handle (left edge of the yellow line) up/down until it lines up with where the floor actually is in the art; that's the height fighters stand at when exported. Dragging it only moves that yellow line itself - platforms, the green jump-reach guide, the background art, and the boundary lines are unaffected, so it's a pure calibration tool. Drag the purple ↕ handles (right edge) to set the top/bottom stage boundaries - the bottom one marks the actual screen bottom of the stage, the top one the screen top, and both can be set independently of the ground line and of each other so stages of any height work. Drag the red ↔ handles to calibrate the side boundaries the same way, for stages of any width - the darkened area shows what's out of bounds. Everything snaps to an 8px grid. Tap empty canvas to add a platform (up to 6). Mark a platform "Invisible" to keep its collision without drawing it, e.g. to line collision up with a background object.
